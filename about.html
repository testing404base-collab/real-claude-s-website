Our Story

Built to make property
feel approachable.

Property360 Nepal opened its doors in Gairidhara, Kathmandu in 2017 — with one idea: buying, selling, renting or managing property in the valley shouldn't require an insider's address book.

We started as a small team with one advantage: we knew the market's blind spots.

Kathmandu's property world has long run on relationships — a friend who knew a friend, a broker who never wrote anything down. Property360 Nepal set out to put a proper consultancy in that gap: real agents, a wide network of realtors across the valley, and a process that doesn't depend on knowing the right person.

We work as matchmakers between the people who own space and the people who need it — evaluating what both sides actually require, rather than pushing whatever's easiest to close.

Alongside the on-the-ground work, we run a digital-first marketing engine — social media, targeted Google campaigns and content — so every listing reaches genuine buyers and tenants, not just foot traffic. Since 2017, that's meant showing up on both sides of a deal: for the family selling their first plot of land, and the tenant trying to find an apartment without paying a broker under the table.
