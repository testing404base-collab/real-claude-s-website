About Us

A go-to place in a market that can feel closed off.

Property360 Nepal was built to make Kathmandu's property market approachable — for the casual homebuyer and the seasoned developer alike.

We work as matchmakers between property owners and the people who need that space, drawing on a wide network of realtors across the valley and a team of agents who handle the details of every transaction directly.

Alongside the on-the-ground work, we run a digital-first marketing engine — social media, targeted Google campaigns and content — so that every listing reaches genuine buyers and tenants, not just foot traffic.

How It Works

From first call to closed deal

A straightforward four-step path, whichever side of the transaction you're on.

01 / Consult

Tell us what you need

Buying, selling, renting or managing — we start with a plain conversation about your goals and budget.

02 / Match

We tap the network

Our realtor network and agents surface properties or buyers that actually fit the brief.

03 / Verify

Visit & review

Site visits, document checks and legal review, so there are no surprises before you commit.

04 / Close

Sign & settle in

We see the paperwork through to close — and stay reachable well after the keys change hands.
